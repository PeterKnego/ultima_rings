# Bake-off v4: a fair SPSC cell at last, and `Park` moves for the first time

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Hardware:** the 4-vCPU VM — which is **2 physical cores with SMT**, a fact none
of v1–v3 knew (`2026-08-12-topology-sweep.md`)
**Method:** built to completion first; five full rounds of every cell in one
session, interleaved by round; criterion mean per cell, reported as
median-of-five with range and spread. Five rounds rather than three because
`README.md` §3 records two cases where a three-round result reversed.

Supersedes `2026-08-11-bakeoff-v3.md`. Run on the 16-core rig the next day
(`2026-08-15-bakeoff-rig.md`), where three of its results do not replicate: the
rtrb tie becomes a 1.67-1.80x lead, the 1.88x MPSC lead becomes a tie at matched
topology, and the sharded prototype goes from 1.68x to 6.20x. Read the two
together.

**Absolutes are not comparable to v3.** crossbeam's MPSC cell read 58.64 on
2026-08-12 and 24.73 today; its SPSC cell read 33.80 in v3 and 8.07 today. The
box is roughly 2.4x slower than it was three days ago on unchanged competitor
code — far beyond the ~20% drift this directory already documents. Compare
ratios within this table and nothing across tables.

## Ranking rule used here

Where two cells' ranges overlap they are reported as a **tie**, not ranked. Given
crossbeam's 22–40% minimum detectable effect
(`2026-08-12-resolution-budgets-rig.md`) and the spreads below, that rule
disposes of two comparisons v3 would have stated as wins.

### This is the first like-for-like SPSC row this project has published

`bakeoff_spsc` had **no cell for this crate** until today. Every "ultima_rings
SPSC" number in v1, v2 and v3 was lifted from `spsc/busy_spin_pipelined` in a
different group — and that cell consumes with `drain(usize::MAX, ..)` while
rtrb, crossbeam, flume and kanal all pop one element per call. Three bake-offs
compared a batched drain against single-item pops without saying so.

This is the same defect found and fixed in `bakeoff_mpsc` earlier in this
session. It was fixed there and not checked for here.

**The correction is smaller than the defect deserved.** `ultima` (single-item
`try_recv`) and `ultima_drain` differ by 1.11x — and in the *opposite* direction
to the assumption: **the batched API is 10% slower**, separated across five
rounds. So v3's SPSC ratios were not meaningfully inflated by the asymmetry. The
asymmetry was still worth removing.

That `drain` loses to `try_recv` on a saturated pipeline is worth a note of its
own. Draining the whole available run amortises the consumer's synchronisation,
but it also lets the ring empty completely before the consumer returns, which
hands the producer a full buffer less often. Not investigated further.

### ultima against rtrb is a tie

334.07 against 325.42 is 1.03x, and the ranges overlap (327.52–342.32 against
316.03–334.18). **A tie, not a win.**

That is the fourth session to measure this pair and the cleanest answer yet:
0.99x (08-06), 0.86x (08-09), 0.91x (08-11), and now 1.03x on a cell that is
finally like-for-like. The honest summary is parity, and the README's softened
claim was right.

`flume`'s 93.1% spread makes it unusable as a point estimate here, joining kanal.

This cell has now produced three different answers on three configurations:
`thingbuf_ref` **1.82x ahead** on this VM on 08-12, **2.07x behind** at 16 cores,
and **tied** here. Same code, same harness, same machine as the first of those.

`README.md` already carries the instruction to quote no direction from this cell.
This is the third data point supporting that, and the first where the
disagreement is with the *same box on a different day*.

| crossbeam-channel blocking | 27.78 | 22.53–28.84 | 28.0% | 1.00x |

**`Park` moves for the first time in four bake-offs: 0.25x in v3, 0.41x now.**

That is the pre-park spin (`6a35383`, tuned to 16 in `6ce792b`) arriving in the
bake-off harness — a **fourth independent harness** after `backoff_isolation`,
the paired gate, and the `PARK_SPINS` sweep. `Park` remains the weakest blocking
path in the roster, and `2026-08-14-backoff-cells.md` says what to do about it:
a caller who wants a blocking API and cares about throughput should choose
`Backoff`, whose blocking cell reaches 38.4 against `Park`'s 12.1.

## What this run does not cover

- **One machine, and a slow day on it.** Every absolute here is ~2.4x below the
  same cells three days ago. Ratios only.
- **2 physical cores.** v1–v3 all ran here believing it was 4 cores. The
  topology sweep shows `BusySpin` and the `String` cell both behave differently
  on real cores, so nothing in this table should be assumed to hold at 16.
- **`flume` SPSC (93.1%) and `kanal` MPSC (45.3%)** are listed for completeness
  and are not point estimates.
- **No `String` cells for `disruptor` or `sharded`**, and no `Backoff`/
  `BackoffYield` cells in the bake-off groups — those live only in
  `backoff_isolation`.
- **`disruptor (take(1))` is still not a like-for-like single-item cell**; see
  the standing caveat in `benches/throughput.rs`.

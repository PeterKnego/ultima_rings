# Bake-off v3: full re-run, and a Park regression the backoff gate missed

> **Superseded by `2026-08-14-bakeoff-v4.md`.** Two things below need flagging:
> the SPSC table's "ultima_rings" row came from a group that consumes with
> `drain`, while every competitor beside it pops one element at a time — v4 adds
> a like-for-like cell and the answer against rtrb becomes a tie. And the Park
> regression this document identified is now fixed: `ultima_park` moved from
> 0.25x to 0.41x of crossbeam's blocking path.

**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Hardware:** 4-core Linux VM, 15 GiB RAM, no swap; box verified by `vmstat` at
87–92% idle, 0–1 runnable, 11 GiB available
**Method:** built to completion first; three full rounds of every cell in one
session; criterion mean per cell, reported as median-of-three with range.

Supersedes `2026-08-09-bakeoff-v2.md`.

## Headline: a 23% Park regression, caused by the CAS backoff

The full re-run put `bakeoff_park_mpsc/ultima_park` at **10.42** (range
10.25–10.49, spread 2.3%), against **14.55** measured on 2026-08-09 before the
backoff landed. No overlap: the earlier minimum, 13.37, is above the current
maximum.

A focused A-B in this session settles causation rather than inferring it across
sessions. The two variants differ **only** in the backoff — both carry the
colocated slot — and were interleaved:

| variant | runs | mean |
|---|---|---:|
| with backoff | 10.30, 10.01, 9.94 | **10.08** |
| without backoff | 12.95, 13.18, 13.14 | **13.09** |

Zero overlap. **The claim-CAS backoff costs `Park` mode 23%**, while it is worth
+108% to +143% to `BusySpin` (`2026-08-11-cas-backoff.md`).

### How this was missed

The backoff's gate ran `mpsc_layout_probe`, which is `BusySpin`-only, across
three configurations, and the head-to-head confirmation used
`mpsc/busy_spin_2_producers` — also `BusySpin`. The `bakeoff_park_mpsc` cell
already existed in the same file and was not run. The gate was thorough within
one wait strategy and blind to the other three.

The design already made this reachable: `Park`'s `try_send` pays a `SeqCst` fence
plus a `consumer_parker.wake()` after every publish (design.md §8). Delaying the
claim delays the publish, which delays the wake, and the extra spinning competes
for cores with a consumer trying to come back. None of that was checked because
no `Park` cell was in the gate.

**Not yet distinguished:** whether the cost attaches to the `Park` *strategy* or
to the blocking `send()` *path*. The Park cell uses `send()`; the `BusySpin`
cells use a `try_send` retry loop. Both differ from each other in two ways at
once, so this measurement cannot separate them.

## The MPSC result confirms on a third harness

`mpsc` at 76.05 against crossbeam's 59.50 is **1.28x**, matching the 1.26x from
the backoff's own confirmation run. Three independent harnesses now agree:
`mpsc_layout_probe`, `mpsc/busy_spin_2_producers` in isolation, and this full
bake-off.

## The 2026-08-09 slowdown was the box, confirmed

`src/spsc.rs` is unchanged across all three sessions:

| | 2026-08-06 | 2026-08-09 | 2026-08-11 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ultima SPSC | 620.10 | 480.65 | 586.93 |
| rtrb | 626.50 | 561.64 | 646.01 |

Both recover together. The 2026-08-09 session was the outlier, and its "~20%
slower on unchanged code" finding holds — that was the machine, not either crate.

## rtrb parity: three sessions, and it does not hold

Ratio of this crate's SPSC to rtrb's: **0.99x** (08-06), **0.86x** (08-09),
**0.91x** (08-11). rtrb leads in two of three sessions, and the one showing
parity is the oldest. The README already carries the softened claim; this is the
third data point and it agrees with the second.

## kanal remains unusable as a point estimate

50.5% spread on the MPSC cell and 18.2% on SPSC, where every other cell except
`disruptor_single` sits under 15%. Its medians are listed for completeness only.

## What this run does not cover

- **Whether the Park regression attaches to the strategy or the blocking path.**
  See above. Needs a `BusySpin`-with-`send()` cell to separate.
- **`Backoff` and `BackoffYield` wait strategies** under the CAS backoff. Neither
  has a bake-off cell, so both are unmeasured against this change. `Backoff` in
  particular parks, so it may share `Park`'s exposure.
- **Producer counts other than 2** in the bake-off cells. The ladder covers 2–64
  for `BusySpin` only (`2026-08-11-producer-ladder.md`).
